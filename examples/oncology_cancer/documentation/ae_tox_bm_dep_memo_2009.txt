BM_DEP in table ae_tox = Bone Marrow DEPression, also called myelosuppression
or bone marrow suppression. It is a haematologic toxicity of chemotherapy: the
bone marrow makes fewer blood cells, so neutrophils (ANC), platelets (PLT) and
haemoglobin (HGB) fall. It is NOT a depression (mood) score.

BM_DEP is an INTEGER grade on the CTCAE severity scale:
  0 = no bone marrow depression
  1 = mild          2 = moderate          3 = severe
  4 = life-threatening (urgent intervention)
  5 = death related to the adverse event
Graded from the cycle's nadir blood count in lab_heme (see ae_tox.LAB_REF).
Grade 3 or higher usually triggers ACT_TKN = DR or DLY.
Bone marrow depression includes neutropenia, thrombocytopenia and anaemia;
FN_FLG marks the febrile neutropenia complication.
