NORTHWIND DATABASE — SCHEMA DOCUMENTATION

Domain overview
---------------
Northwind is the operational database of a small wholesale trading company,
"Northwind Traders," that imports specialty food and beverage products from
suppliers around the world and resells them to customers. The database records
the company's product catalogue, its suppliers and customers, the staff who
handle sales, the shippers who deliver the goods, and every customer order
together with its individual line items. It is a compact but complete
small-business enterprise resource planning (ERP) schema covering purchasing,
inventory, sales, and shipping.


TABLE: categories
-----------------
Groups the products sold by Northwind into food categories such as Beverages,
Condiments, Confections, Dairy Products, and Seafood. Each product belongs to
exactly one category. Categories organise the catalogue for browsing and
reporting.

  categoryID    Unique identifier of the category. Primary key.
  categoryName  Short human-readable name of the category (e.g. "Beverages").
  description   A longer free-text description of the kinds of products the
                category contains.


TABLE: suppliers
----------------
The companies from which Northwind purchases the products it resells. A supplier
provides one or more products. This is a business partner on the purchasing
(inbound) side of the company.

  supplierID    Unique identifier of the supplier. Primary key.
  companyName   Legal or trading name of the supplier company.
  contactName   Name of the individual contact person at the supplier.
  contactTitle  Job title of that contact person (e.g. "Purchasing Manager").
  address       Street address of the supplier.
  city          City of the supplier.
  region        State, province, or region of the supplier; may be empty.
  postalCode    Postal or ZIP code of the supplier.
  country       Country of the supplier.
  phone         Telephone number of the supplier.
  fax           Fax number of the supplier; may be empty.
  homePage      URL or reference to the supplier's home page; may be empty.


TABLE: shippers
---------------
The freight companies that Northwind uses to deliver customer orders. Each order
is shipped by one of these carriers.

  shipperID     Unique identifier of the shipping company. Primary key.
  companyName   Name of the shipping company (e.g. "Speedy Express").
  phone         Telephone number of the shipping company.


TABLE: customers
----------------
The companies that buy products from Northwind. A customer places one or more
orders. This is a business partner on the sales (outbound) side of the company.

  customerID    Unique five-character code identifying the customer. Primary key.
                Unlike most identifiers in this database it is a text code
                (e.g. "ALFKI"), not a number.
  companyName   Legal or trading name of the customer company.
  contactName   Name of the individual contact person at the customer.
  contactTitle  Job title of that contact person (e.g. "Sales Representative").
  address       Street address of the customer.
  city          City of the customer.
  region        State, province, or region of the customer; may be empty.
  postalCode    Postal or ZIP code of the customer.
  country       Country of the customer.
  phone         Telephone number of the customer.
  fax           Fax number of the customer; may be empty.


TABLE: employees
----------------
The staff of Northwind Traders, primarily the sales representatives who take and
manage customer orders. Employees are organised in a management hierarchy: an
employee may report to another employee (their supervisor).

  employeeID       Unique identifier of the employee. Primary key.
  lastName         Family name of the employee.
  firstName        Given name of the employee.
  title            Job title of the employee (e.g. "Sales Representative",
                   "Vice President, Sales").
  titleOfCourtesy  Courtesy title used when addressing the employee
                   (e.g. "Ms.", "Dr.").
  birthDate        Date of birth of the employee.
  hireDate         Date the employee was hired by the company.
  address          Home street address of the employee.
  city             Home city of the employee.
  region           Home state, province, or region of the employee; may be empty.
  postalCode       Home postal or ZIP code of the employee.
  country          Home country of the employee.
  homePhone        Home telephone number of the employee.
  extension        Internal office telephone extension number of the employee.
  notes            Free-text biographical notes about the employee's background,
                   education, and skills.
  reportsTo        The employeeID of this employee's supervisor. This is a
                   self-reference into the employees table: it links an employee
                   to their manager, defining the reporting hierarchy. May be
                   empty for employees at the top of the hierarchy.
  photoPath        URL or file path of a photograph of the employee.


TABLE: products
---------------
The goods that Northwind buys from suppliers and sells to customers. Each product
is supplied by one supplier and belongs to one category. This table also holds
current inventory and pricing information for each product.

  productID        Unique identifier of the product. Primary key.
  productName      Name of the product (e.g. "Chai", "Aniseed Syrup").
  supplierID       The supplier that provides this product. References suppliers.
  categoryID       The category this product belongs to. References categories.
  quantityPerUnit  Free-text description of the packaging and unit of sale, i.e.
                   how the product is packaged for one unit of ordering
                   (e.g. "10 boxes x 20 bags", "24 - 12 oz bottles").
  unitPrice        The current selling price for one unit of the product, in the
                   company's currency.
  unitsInStock     The number of units currently held in inventory and available
                   to sell.
  unitsOnOrder     The number of units that have been ordered from the supplier
                   but not yet received into inventory.
  reorderLevel     The inventory threshold at which the product should be
                   reordered from the supplier; when unitsInStock falls to this
                   level, stock should be replenished.
  discontinued     Boolean flag indicating whether the product has been
                   discontinued and is no longer offered for sale.


TABLE: orders
-------------
The header record for each customer order: who placed it, which employee handled
it, when it was placed and shipped, how it was shipped, and where it was sent.
The individual products on the order are held in the order_details table.

  orderID         Unique identifier of the order. Primary key.
  customerID      The customer that placed the order. References customers.
  employeeID      The employee who handled the order. References employees.
  orderDate       The date the order was placed.
  requiredDate    The date by which the customer required delivery.
  shippedDate     The date the order was actually shipped; may be empty if not
                  yet shipped.
  shipVia         The shipping company used to deliver the order. References
                  shippers (via its shipperID).
  freight         The freight (shipping) cost charged for the order.
  shipName        Name of the recipient the order is shipped to.
  shipAddress     Street address the order is shipped to.
  shipCity        City the order is shipped to.
  shipRegion      State, province, or region the order is shipped to; may be empty.
  shipPostalCode  Postal or ZIP code the order is shipped to.
  shipCountry     Country the order is shipped to.


TABLE: order_details
--------------------
The individual line items of an order. Each row represents one product on one
order, together with the price, quantity, and discount agreed for that line. This
is a junction table linking orders to products: its primary key is the
combination of orderID and productID, and it carries its own attributes
describing the sale of that product on that order.

  orderID     The order this line item belongs to. Part of the primary key.
              References orders.
  productID   The product being sold on this line. Part of the primary key.
              References products.
  unitPrice   The price per unit charged for the product on this order line. This
              may differ from the product's current catalogue unitPrice, because
              it records the price at the time the order was placed.
  quantity    The number of units of the product ordered on this line.
  discount    The discount applied to this line, expressed as a fraction between
              0 and 1 (for example, 0.15 means a 15 percent discount).


Notes on relationships
----------------------
- A category has many products; a product has one category.
- A supplier supplies many products; a product has one supplier.
- A customer places many orders; an order has one customer.
- An employee handles many orders; an order has one employee.
- A shipper delivers many orders; an order is shipped by one shipper.
- An order has many order_details lines; each line belongs to one order.
- A product appears on many order_details lines; each line refers to one product.
- An employee may report to another employee (reportsTo), forming a management
  hierarchy within the employees table.
