# Music Store — Glossary

Brief definitions of domain terms, with the standard concept each corresponds to
so that mappings and alignments can be made.

- **Artist** — the creator of recorded music. Corresponds to the general notion
  of a music group or performing act ("music artist" / "music group").
- **Album** — a published collection of recordings ("music album" / "music
  release").
- **Track** — an individual recording on an album ("music recording" /
  "sound recording"). The terms *track* and *recording* are used
  interchangeably here.
- **Release date** — the publication date of an album.
- **Duration** — how long a track plays, expressed in milliseconds in this
  dataset.
- **Unit price** — the per-track retail price, a monetary amount.

These definitions are intended to help align the store's tables to widely used
music and commerce vocabularies (for example, concepts named *MusicArtist*,
*MusicAlbum*, and *MusicRecording*, and a commerce notion of a priced item).
